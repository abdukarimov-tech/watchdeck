WatchDeck — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-10-27

1. Summary
WatchDeck is a self-hosted-first monitoring client. We designed it so that your
server data never has to pass through servers we control.

2. Data stored on your device
- Relay address and client tokens for the servers you add. Tokens are stored in
  the app's private storage and never leave the device except to authenticate
  against your own relay over TLS.
- App preferences (active server, language follows your device).

3. Data processed by your relay
- Metrics (CPU, memory, disk, network), container states and logs are transmitted
  between the agent on your server and your devices through your relay
  (self-hosted or operated by whoever deployed it for you).
- The relay stores an alert history in its local SQLite database and route
  metadata (which agent is connected). It does not need to persist your metrics.

4. Push notifications
- If you enable alerts, the relay sends event notifications through Google's
  Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Google processes the delivery; the message
  payload contains only event metadata (server label, container name, event kind).
- Your FCM device token is stored on your relay to address your device.

5. Data we do not collect
- The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
- We do not receive your metrics, logs, tokens or server addresses.

6. Permissions
- INTERNET: to talk to your relay.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show downtime alerts (Android 13+). The app requests
  this permission at first launch; you may decline and grant it later.

7. Your controls
- "Reset app data" in Settings removes all servers and tokens from the device.
- Removing a server in the server menu deletes its credentials from the device.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

8. Children
WatchDeck is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes
This policy may be updated with the app. The "Last updated" date above reflects
the current version.

10. Contact
